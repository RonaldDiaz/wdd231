Overview

Site Name

The name of the site is Ready 3D. This name represents the agility and preparedness of the 3D printing service, stating that the business is ready to provide robust solutions and custom fabrication. It is coupled with a geometric cube logo to reinforce the technical nature of the brand.

Domain availability: The URL https://www.3dready.com/ is available.

Site Purpose

The site is as a digital storefront and interactive catalog for Ready 3D. It is a platform to showcase 3D-printed utilities, electronic shells, templates, and molds. Also, it offers a contact process for clients to request custom modeling and specialized printing services.

Scenarios

These are the primary questions our target audience (makers, engineers, and DIY enthusiasts) will ask when visiting the site:

  • Scenario 1: What are the exact dimensions, materials, and technical specifications for the Arduino electronic enclosures?
  • Scenario 2: How can I contact the team to request a custom 3D printed prototype from my own CAD file?
  • Scenario 3: Are there pre-designed functional tools and baking molds available for immediate purchase in the catalog?
